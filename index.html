Building Jaunty

I built Jaunty around the timetable I used as a student. Friends began using it too, which led me to continue developing the app as an independent developer.

From screenshot to timetable

On-device text recognition reads timetable screenshots. A review step gives users a chance to check and correct the draft before it becomes part of their schedule. Optional on-device AI can assist on supported devices. Timetable data stays on the device, with optional private iCloud sync.

Built with Swift · SwiftUI · SwiftData · CloudKit · Apple Vision · Foundation Models · WidgetKit · ActivityKit
